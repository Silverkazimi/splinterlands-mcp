# Land balance history paging and income — 2026-10-21

Fresh public GET observations against `https://vapi.splinterlands.com/land/resources/balances/history/{player}` and its `/count` route. The observed account was the maintainer's own account. The retained fixture uses `player_owner` in place of account names. No Hive history was read.

- Unfiltered `limit=1` returned one 945-byte row; `limit=3&offset=0` returned three 2,805-byte rows. The newest row was `id=40081520`, `created_date=2026-10-20T06:23:30.000Z`. `limit=3&offset=1` returned `data:[]`, though the unfiltered `/count` returned 1,290. Thus `limit` is honored for the measured small values, and positive `offset` is not a usable row cursor. There is no observed cursor parameter.
- `startDate=2026-10-20&endDate=2026-10-20` returned no rows; the same start with `endDate=2026-10-21` had count 300. This is consistent with a half-open date range. A one-minute window `06:23:00Z`–`06:24:00Z` had count 21, and `limit=3` returned three rows all at `06:23:30Z`. Timestamp filters therefore narrow the set, but a group sharing one timestamp cannot be split with time filters alone.
- The count for 2026-10-20 was 300. Six consecutive four-hour count windows returned 0, 300, 0, 0, 0, 0. Full-day list and full-day `limit=100` calls timed out after 24 seconds; a one-minute `limit=21` call timed out after 12 seconds. `limit=200` returned HTTP 400. The upstream bottleneck remains even below the 2 MiB transport cap.
- Daily counts for 2026-10-19, 18, 17, 16 were 283, 3, 358, 273. The three October 18 rows are retained in `tests/evidence/land-income-2026-10-18.json` with account names replaced. All three were `resource_id=1` and had `swap_tokens` DEC legs; none is evidence of production income.
- The row fields were `id`, `region_number`, `player`, `amount`, `end_balance`, `operation_id`, `resource_id`, `trx_id`, `created_date`, `balance_history`, `counterparty`. Nested legs had `player`, `token`, `amount`, `balance_start`, `balance_end`, `block_num`, `trx_id`, `type`, `created_date`, `counterparty`, `last_update_date`. A fresh `GET /land/deeds?player={player}&limit=1000` paired resource IDs and symbols: 1 GRAIN, 2 SPS, 3 RESEARCH, 5 WOOD, 6 STONE, 7 IRON. Other IDs are kept numeric unless independently mapped.

**Implication.** Date windows can replace broken offsets for bounded reads. A page is complete only when its count equals its returned unique rows; a day that times out, exhausts the call budget or has too many rows at one timestamp must be marked incomplete. The `amount` field is a balance movement, not automatically minted income. Unrecognized leg types remain `other`; current deed production is shown separately as a rate estimate, not assigned to historical days.

## Review probes (2026-10-21)

Bounded public reads of the same owner account found October 19 harvest rows with `land_player_resource_mint`, `land_uncaptured_tax_mint`, `land_resource_castle_tax_mint`, and `land_resource_keep_tax_mint` legs. Their token was GRAIN and their amounts included both positive player mint and negative tax movements. October 12 also returned an IRON mint event. Redacted leading-row projections are in `tests/evidence/land-income-leg-types-2026-10-21.json`. October 20 and 18 leading rows had `swap_tokens`; October 17 and 15 had mixed `dec_stake` and Land mint legs. This sample did not confirm a transfer or ordinary spend leg type. No transfer or spend classification is claimed.

A bounded `GET /land/deeds?player={owner}&limit=1000` returned 131 deeds with resource ID/symbol pairs including 1/GRAIN, 2/SPS, 3/RESEARCH, 5/WOOD, 6/STONE and 7/IRON. The active worksite `token_symbol` matched its deed's `resource_symbol` in the retained samples. `total_work_per_hour` closely matched `worksite_details.rewards_per_hour` for sampled STONE, IRON and RESEARCH sites and their projected receipts were of the same hourly order. SPS differed (7.872 work/hour against 7.431 rewards/hour), so `total_work_per_hour × 24` is labeled a current deed work-rate estimate, not guaranteed received tokens or realized income. Redacted numeric evidence is in `tests/evidence/land-income-rate-units-2026-10-21.json`.

The raw history page uses one-minute windows. An incomplete minute now returns `skip_cursor` at its end, allowing callers to continue with an explicit gap. Skipping does not make coverage complete. Even an empty minute needs a count read, so traversing a 14-day range can require 20,160 page calls.

The transfer probe extended the same bounded read to daily leading pages from 2026-09-15 through 2026-10-08. The September 19 row with operation_id 23 and amount -15,000 GRAIN has an empty `balance_history` and a counterparty, but the response does not identify a transfer leg type. It is retained as a redacted projection in `tests/evidence/land-income-transfer-probe-2026-10-21.json` and remains `other`. Other sampled legs included `add_liquidity` and `keep_tax_mint_overflow`; these were not promoted into accounting categories.
