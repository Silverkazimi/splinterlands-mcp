# Collection max-level rule — 2026-10-26

The official [Combining Cards](https://docs.splinterlands.com/cards/combining-cards) guide says BCX requirements vary by set/edition, rarity, and foil. The official [Gold Foil Cards](https://docs.splinterlands.com/cards/gold-foil-cards) guide documents lower gold BCX requirements. The official [Black Foils and Variants FAQ](https://support.splinterlands.com/hc/en-us/articles/47499969805844-Black-Foils-and-Variants-FAQ) says black foils are issued at maximum level.

The public `GET https://api2.splinterlands.com/settings` response captured in `tests/fixtures/metadata-settings.fixture.json` carries rarity-indexed `combine_rates` with 10/8/6/4 levels and `foundations_combine_rates` with 5/4/3/2 levels, plus gold and Soulkeep-specific tables. This server compares each copy's BCX to the last rarity-specific threshold from the appropriate regular or gold table. Foundations uses its shorter table. Alpha and Beta thresholds are derived from the same settings XP regime; shared definition IDs are classified by the copy's `card_set`, not the definition's edition list. Black foil codes 3 and 4 are maxed at issue. A copy above the threshold stays maxed and reports positive `excess_bcx`. `max_level` comes from the definition's level-array length where available, or the rarity cap. Definitions with no reliable cap and copies with no definition return null for `max_level` and `maxed`.

The collection route supplies its full collection as one `cards[]` response, without upstream paging or filters. This server streams the full response, applies `maxed` and `min_land_base_pp` locally before paging, and preserves its existing page and result-size limits. On collections within its 25,000-card index limit, later filtered pages reuse the short-lived index. Above that limit, a different filter or page re-streams the upstream response.

No live upstream call was made for this change. The manager's live check should compare representative regular, gold, black, Foundations, and older-set copies against the game's shown level before release.
